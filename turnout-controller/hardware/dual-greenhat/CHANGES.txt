CHANGES to the IoTT GreenHat Coil Driver rev 1.0
================================================

Upstream: IoTT GreenHat Coil Driver, rev 1.0, Hans R. Tanner (IoTT),
2021-07-11 / 2021-08-27, from tanner87661/IoTTStick,
"Hat Devices/GreenHat Power Extension" (copy in upstream/).

Derived design: CikutRail dual-GreenHat carrier board, a Stamp-S3Bat DIP
carrier with six turnout channels for Kato Unitrack turnouts.

These modifications are licensed under the TAPR Open Hardware License
(www.tapr.org/OHL).

The upstream files are unchanged ("before" versions, in upstream/). The
new files ("after" versions) are:
  DESIGN.md                            design note and rationale
  easyeda/build_schematic.py           generator for the schematic
  easyeda/DualGreenHat_schematic.json  EasyEDA Standard schematic source
  easyeda/netlist.txt                  netlist (ASCII)
  easyeda/bom.csv                      bill of materials (ASCII)
  easyeda/lib/*.json                   EasyEDA/LCSC library symbols used

2026-09-28  Perry Ismangil
  Circuit
  - Two copies of the GreenHat circuit on one board: six channels.
  - Removed the steering diodes D1-D6 and output terminal pins 3-4
    (twin-coil turnouts). Each turnout uses 2 pins for its two-wire
    Kato coil, on two 6-way terminals (KF128-2.54-6P), one per block.
  - Removed the input headers J1-J3. The inputs are routed to a socket
    for an M5Stack Stamp-S3Bat DIP (G1-G6).
  - Removed the H7350 LDO and J6. The 5 V logic rail comes from the
    S3Bat's switched 5VOUT.
  - Replaced the reverse-polarity circuit (Q1 SI2301CDS, D7, R7) with one
    SS34 Schottky diode, and added a 2 A PTC resettable fuse at the input.
    The design assumes a fixed, regulated 12 V supply.
  - Added a TPS563201 12 V -> 5 V buck converter feeding the S3Bat's 5VIN,
    so one terminal powers everything.
  - 74HC86 -> 74HCT86 (valid logic levels from 3.3 V GPIOs).
  - 10.2 kOhm -> 10 kOhm for the input pull-ups and RC series resistors.
  - DRV8313 support parts changed to the TI datasheet values: charge-pump
    capacitor 100 nF -> 10 nF, V3P3OUT capacitor 4.7 nF -> 470 nF;
    100 nF per VM pin plus 22 uF bulk per driver pair.
  - Logic GND and power GND are one net, joined on the board.
  - Removed the 1 MOhm pulse-length trimmers R3/R4/R6. They were set fully
    counter-clockwise (0 Ohm), so the pulse is fixed by the 10 kOhm series
    resistor and the 10 uF capacitor.
  Documentation
  - New schematic generated for EasyEDA Standard (see above).
  - New PCB placement for EasyEDA Standard, 70 x 56 mm, not yet routed
    (easyeda/build_pcb.py, easyeda/DualGreenHat_pcb.json,
    easyeda/placement.svg, footprints in easyeda/fp/). Gerbers and PDF
    exports will be added here when made.

2026-11-19  Perry Ismangil
  Circuit
  - Turnout terminals renumbered against the channels: J101 pins 1-2 are
    channel 3, 3-4 channel 2, 5-6 channel 1 (J201: channels 6, 5, 4). In
    each pair the lower pin is the delayed side (B), the higher the direct
    side (A).
  - Added R5, a 0 Ohm link (0805) between VM and the buck converter's
    input. The buck's input capacitors C3-C5 are now behind it, on the new
    net VM_BUCK.
  PCB placement
  - Drivers placed in the order U203, U202, U103, U102 and the terminals
    moved over them, so the coil traces don't cross.
  - Buck converter parts moved to the pins they serve: U1 rotated 90
    degrees, C3 across VIN and GND with room for the SW via, C6 at BST,
    R1-R3 at EN and FB, L1 and C7/C8 at SW (TPS563201 datasheet, figure
    7-18).
  - Placement sketch: Stamp-S3Bat outline corrected (28.0 mm PCB plus the
    USB-C shell).

2026-11-20  Perry Ismangil
  Circuit
  - Buck output capacitors C7/C8 changed from 22 uF 0805 (C45783) to the
    22 uF 1206 part used for the bulk capacitors (C12891).
  - Buck feedback divider changed from 100 kOhm / 18 kOhm to the
    datasheet's 54.9 kOhm / 10 kOhm (4.98 V): R2 is now 54.9 kOhm 0603
    (C23077), R3 10 kOhm 0402 (C25744).
  - Added five digital sensor inputs on the spare GPIOs G7-G11: one open
    3-pin 2.54 mm pin header each (J4-J8), with a 1 kOhm series resistor
    (R6-R10).
  - Added an I2C port on G10 (SDA) and G11 (SCL): a 4-pin JST SH connector
    in the Qwiic / STEMMA QT order (J9, SM04B-SRSS-TB) with 4.7 kOhm
    pull-ups (R11, R12). G10 and G11 serve either J7/J8 or J9.
  - Added a 3.3 V regulator for the sensor connectors (U2, AMS1117-3.3,
    with C9-C11), fed from the buck converter's 5 V.
  PCB placement
  - C7, C8 and R4 moved to make room for the 1206 capacitors; R2 and R3
    re-placed above the FB pin.
  - J4/J5 side by side on the left edge, J6 and U2 on the right, J7 above
    J8 and then J9 on the bottom edge between the mounting holes.

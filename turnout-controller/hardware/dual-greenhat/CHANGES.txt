CHANGES to the IoTT GreenHat Coil Driver rev 1.0
================================================

Upstream: IoTT GreenHat Coil Driver, rev 1.0, Hans R. Tanner (IoTT),
2021-07-11 / 2021-08-27, from tanner87661/IoTTStick,
"Hat Devices/GreenHat Power Extension" (copy in upstream/).

Derived design: CikutRail dual-GreenHat carrier board, a Stamp-S3Bat DIP
carrier with six turnout channels for Kato Unitrack turnouts.

(www.tapr.org/OHL).

The upstream files are unchanged ("before" versions, in upstream/). The
new files ("after" versions) are:

  easyeda/build_schematic.py           generator for the schematic
  easyeda/DualGreenHat_schematic.json  EasyEDA Standard schematic source
  easyeda/netlist.txt                  netlist (ASCII)
  easyeda/bom.csv                      bill of materials (ASCII)
  easyeda/lib/*.json                   EasyEDA/LCSC library symbols used

2026-09-28  Perry Ismangil
  Circuit
  - Two copies of the GreenHat circuit on one board: six channels.
  - Removed the steering diodes D1-D6 and output terminal pins 3-4
    (twin-coil turnouts). Each turnout uses 2 pins for its two-wire
    Kato coil, on two 6-way terminals (KF128-2.54-6P), one per block.
  - Removed the input headers J1-J3. The inputs are routed to a socket
    for an M5Stack Stamp-S3Bat DIP (G1-G6).
  - Removed the H7350 LDO and J6. The 5 V logic rail comes from the
    S3Bat's switched 5VOUT.
  - Replaced the reverse-polarity circuit (Q1 SI2301CDS, D7, R7) with one
    SS34 Schottky diode, and added a 2 A PTC resettable fuse at the input.
    The design assumes a fixed, regulated 12 V supply.
  - Added a TPS563201 12 V -> 5 V buck converter feeding the S3Bat's 5VIN,
    so one terminal powers everything.
  - 74HC86 -> 74HCT86 (valid logic levels from 3.3 V GPIOs).
  - 10.2 kOhm -> 10 kOhm for the input pull-ups and RC series resistors.
  - DRV8313 support parts changed to the TI datasheet values: charge-pump
    capacitor 100 nF -> 10 nF, V3P3OUT capacitor 4.7 nF -> 470 nF;
    100 nF per VM pin plus 22 uF bulk per driver pair.
  - Logic GND and power GND are one net, joined on the board.
  - Removed the 1 MOhm pulse-length trimmers R3/R4/R6. They were set fully
    counter-clockwise (0 Ohm), so the pulse is fixed by the 10 kOhm series
    resistor and the 10 uF capacitor.
  Documentation
  - New schematic generated for EasyEDA Standard (see above); no PCB
    layout or Gerbers yet. Those will be added here when made, together
    with PDF exports of the schematic.
